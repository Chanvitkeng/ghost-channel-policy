1. Introduction

Ghost Channel ("we", "our", or "the application") is an automated content creation and publishing tool. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when using our TikTok integration.

2. Information We Access

When connecting to TikTok, we request the following permissions:

  • user.info.basic — To verify account identity
  • video.publish — To publish video content to TikTok on behalf of the account owner
  • video.upload — To upload video files to TikTok

3. How We Use Information

We use TikTok account access solely to:

  • Upload and publish short video content (Thai ghost story videos) to the connected TikTok account
  • Retrieve basic account information to confirm successful authentication

We do not sell, share, or transfer any account information to third parties.

4. Data Storage

We store only the OAuth access tokens required to authenticate with TikTok's API. These tokens are stored securely on our private server and are never shared with any third party. Tokens are used exclusively to post content to the authorized TikTok account.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained until the account owner revokes access or the token expires. No other personal data is retained.

6. User Rights

You may revoke the application's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's account settings under "Manage app permissions". Upon revocation, all stored tokens are invalidated.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect stored credentials. Access tokens are stored in environment variables on a secured private server.
